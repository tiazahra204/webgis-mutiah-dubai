Project Overview

An interactive thematic WebGIS visualizing Deira, Dubai's land use classification pattern and public facility points. Developed with a high-contrast pink/magenta cartographic scheme for optimal legibility.

Location Overview

Deira is the historical heart of Dubai, UAE. Bordered by the Persian Gulf and the Dubai Creek, this 143 sq km district represents a key commercial and cultural sector characterized by traditional souks, dense residential blocks, and vital public infrastructure.
